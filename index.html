What I actually do

Most of my work sits between hardware and software. At Landis+Gyr I work on the E470 smart meter, writing automated test sequences in NI TestStand and Robot Framework so that meters get validated without someone sitting at a bench pressing buttons for hours.

The other half is full-stack. I build and maintain services for our internal MDE/Volda platform — a C# backend, an Angular frontend, and Python FastAPI services running on GCP and Kubernetes. Alongside that I trained custom YOLOv5 and YOLOv8 models that read meter LCD screens directly from camera images, which is now part of how the meters get checked.

I joined as a consultant in October 2024 and converted to a full-time engineer six months later. Before that I was at GAOTek, where I started as a developer intern and moved into leading a small team.
